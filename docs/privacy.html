DIV EDIT AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

DIV EDIT AI is a mobile video editing application developed by DivStudio. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled by the app

  • Media you select: Photos, videos and audio you import are used to provide editing and export features. The app does not need to upload your media to DivStudio merely to edit it.
  • Project information: Editing projects and settings may be stored locally on your device so you can continue your work.
  • Payments: If paid Premium features are enabled, payment processing may be provided by a third-party payment processor. Payment card or bank credentials are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by DIV EDIT AI.

How information is used

Information is used to operate editing features, save your projects, process exports, provide requested Premium functionality, maintain security, and improve reliability.

Sharing

DivStudio does not sell your personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide a requested service, process a payment, comply with law, or protect the security of the service.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored projects and media can be removed by deleting them from the app or device. If a future account or cloud service stores information, deletion requests can be made using the contact below.

Children

DIV EDIT AI is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Security

DivStudio uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact divstudio03@gmail.com.
