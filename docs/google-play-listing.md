## Short description

## Full description
Create, edit and export cinematic videos with DIV EDIT AI by DivStudio.

DIV EDIT AI brings professional mobile editing tools into one creative workspace. Import your videos, photos and audio, arrange clips on a timeline, trim and split footage, add text, adjust color and audio, apply effects and transitions, and export your finished video.

### Create faster
- Multi-track editing workflow
- Trim, split, merge and rearrange clips
- Text, filters, transitions and effects
- Speed and keyframe controls
- Audio and music editing
- MP4 export workflow

### Hollywood-style creative tools
Build cinematic scenes with visual effects such as fire, lightning, smoke, particles, portals, teleport-style effects, cloning and other compositing tools.

### AI filmmaking
Use AI-assisted creative workflows to help plan and shape scenes and storytelling while keeping the editing process in your hands.

DIV EDIT AI is made for creators, filmmakers, animators, advertisers, social-media creators and businesses.

Built by DivStudio.
